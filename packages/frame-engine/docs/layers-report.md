## Interpretation

Engine-side error rows: **0**. Every known-difference reason names the concrete ffmpeg/render-path operation active in that isolated window; classes are not accepted merely because a reason string exists.

Blend modes `difference`, `darken`, `lighten`, `overlay`, `hardlight`, and `softlight` are verified by two render-cut sample times each, in addition to `screen` and `multiply`. `add` is verified at the transfer-function level with ffmpeg's `addition` mode, but render-cut cannot render the edit vocabulary entry and is reported explicitly instead of being silently skipped.
