# Researching an English-language market

Set the target geography and English-speaking audience before choosing platforms or search terms. Check public channels and videos in the target market, including upload frequency, format, duration, titles, and thumbnails. Use accessible first-party audience data where the project owns a channel; YouTube Studio's Audience and Trends views can reveal viewing formats, geographies, search interests, and possible content gaps. Access and availability vary by account and region, so record what was actually visible and when.

The current schema still requires `target.japan_sns`. For an English-language brief, fill its `platforms` and `notes` with clearly labeled evidence for the actual target market rather than inventing Japanese-market observations. Record public URLs and access dates in `sources[]`; if free sources provide no evidence, say so in `notes` with the reason. Keep paid listening tools behind the existing approval gate.
